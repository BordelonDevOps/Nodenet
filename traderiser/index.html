What is being tested

This longer observation will show how this specific live test develops across more market conditions. It will not prove that the method works in other markets or with larger accounts.

TradeRiser logic

TradeRiser discovers a liquid ETF candidate set from provider data and evaluates completed market sessions. A fund must be above its 200-day average and have positive three-month and six-month momentum to qualify. Eligible funds are ranked using twelve-to-one momentum, which omits the latest month, then filtered to avoid highly correlated duplicates and sized using covariance-aware risk contribution.

The live policy allows up to ten positions and blocks borrowed leverage and short selling. Monitoring runs at 9:15 AM Central on market days. Each optional replacement is tested as an individual sell-to-buy pair for persistence, historical relative evidence, costs, estimated taxes, and uncertainty. Qualified pairs are staged within the live order limits; severe risk exits remain independent.

How agentic trading is used

The agentic process reads the account, positions, open orders, and fresh quotes. It runs the TradeRiser logic, checks the proposed change against the live policy, and submits an order only if every check passes. It then reads the broker results again, records fills and fees, and verifies the audit journal.

A separate challenger tests other ideas in shadow mode. Its trades remain hypothetical and cannot reach the brokerage account.
