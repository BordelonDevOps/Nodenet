What is being tested

Twenty-one days will show how this specific test performed. It will not prove that the method works in other markets or with larger accounts.

TradeRiser logic

TradeRiser evaluates a fixed list of broad-market funds using completed market sessions. A fund must be above its 200-day average and have positive three-month and six-month momentum to qualify. Eligible funds are ranked by volatility-adjusted momentum, filtered to avoid highly correlated duplicates, and sized using covariance-aware risk contribution.

The live policy allows up to five positions, blocks borrowed leverage and short selling, and ignores small changes inside the rebalance band. Monitoring runs at 9:15 AM Central on market days. Routine rebalancing occurs monthly, while a severe trend-and-momentum failure can trigger an earlier exit.

How agentic trading is used

The agentic process reads the account, positions, open orders, and fresh quotes. It runs the TradeRiser logic, checks the proposed change against the live policy, and submits an order only if every check passes. It then reads the broker results again, records fills and fees, and verifies the audit journal.

A separate challenger tests other ideas in shadow mode. Its trades remain hypothetical and cannot reach the brokerage account.
